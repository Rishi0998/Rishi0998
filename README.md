## About

I am a **cybersecurity engineer and AI security researcher** currently pursuing a B.E. in Computer Science and Cybersecurity at Chandigarh University (2025–2029). My work sits at the intersection of offensive security and AI safety — building systems that defend against emerging adversarial threats in both traditional networks and large language models.

I compete internationally on [CTFtime.org](https://ctftime.org), ranked in the **global top 50**, and have won national-level competitions including CyberStrike CTF and the ZEAI Made in India Hackathon. Alongside my technical work, I manage a digital content network with over **3 million subscribers**.

## Projects

### 🛡️ Protocol X — Zero-Trust AI Firewall

A security perimeter engineered for large language models and GenAI systems. Protects against prompt injections, jailbreaks, and data exfiltration using a multi-stage detection pipeline combining regex filtering, heuristic anomaly scoring, and semantic intent analysis.

**Role:** Sole architect and developer &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stack:** `Python` `NLP` `AI Safety` `Zero Trust Architecture`

---

### 🔍 AI Forensic Time Machine

An AI-powered log forensics and incident reconstruction system for blue-team operators. Ingests raw server, firewall, and OS event logs, detects attack patterns automatically, builds a visual intrusion timeline, and generates an executive triage report with remediation guidance.

**Role:** Sole architect and developer &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stack:** `Python` `Cyber Forensics` `Log Analysis` `Incident Response`

---

### 🤖 Nextron — Intelligent IoT Vehicle

A hardware-software integrated autonomous vehicle supporting five input modalities: hand gesture detection, voice recognition, Bluetooth SPP control, ultrasonic obstacle avoidance, and manual override. Embedded firmware written in C/C++ for real-time sensor fusion and low-latency response.

**Role:** Lead hardware and firmware engineer &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stack:** `Embedded C/C++` `IoT` `Arduino` `Sensors` `Bluetooth`

---

### 📺 Digital Media Ecosystem — 3M+ Subscribers

Scaled a digital content network from zero to over three million subscribers using data-driven content strategy, CTR and retention optimization, and cross-platform audience funnelling. Involved algorithmic behaviour analysis and audience psychology research.

**Role:** Founder and operator &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Stack:** `Growth Analytics` `Content Strategy` `SEO` `Audience Psychology`
